The funding

What the NSW Home Energy Saver Program actually is

A NSW Government program that helps households pay for energy-saving upgrades, including heat pump hot water. The route we work with is the zero-interest loan, delivered by Brighte. You borrow at 0% and repay over up to ten years.

Who qualifies

NSW homeowners and landlords

The property must be in NSW. Social housing and short-stay accommodation aren’t eligible.

Questions

Before you enquire

Is $599 what I’ll actually pay?

No. $599 + GST is a starting price. What you pay depends on your property and what the installation involves. Ecogenica gives you a firm price before you agree to anything.

Who does the installation?

Ecogenica Pty Ltd, holding NSW contractor licence 397621C. ESG (Energy Savers Group) handles the advertising and passes your enquiry across. ESG does not supply, contract or install the system.

Am I guaranteed to be eligible?

No. Eligibility is set by the NSW Government and depends on your household income, whether you own the property, and whether your home suits the upgrade. That’s exactly what the eligibility check is for.

Do I have to take the finance?

No. The zero-interest loan is an option for eligible households, not a condition. Note the minimum loan is $2,000, so a smaller job can fall below the threshold.

Will someone knock on my door?

No. Unsolicited door-knocking to promote these upgrades is banned under the NSW program. You’ll only hear from us because you asked to.

ESG (Energy Savers Group) provides advertising and lead generation only. All supply, contracting and installation work is carried out by Ecogenica Pty Ltd.
