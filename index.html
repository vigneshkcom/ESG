The rebate

How you actually get the rebate

You don’t. Not in the sense of filling in a form and waiting for money back. The government discount for a heat pump hot water upgrade is applied at the point of sale, which means it has already been taken off every price on this page. Separately, if your job is large enough, a 0% interest NSW Home Energy Saver Loan is available.

The discount

Already deducted from the prices above

Heat pump hot water upgrades qualify for discounts under the NSW Energy Savings Scheme and Peak Demand Reduction Scheme. These are claimed by an accredited provider on your behalf and come straight off the installed price. No form, no receipts, no waiting for a refund.

Questions

Before you enquire

Is the advertised price what I’ll actually pay?

It’s a starting price for a straight electric-to-electric swap, with the government discount already deducted. What you pay depends on your property and what the installation involves. Gas and solar-boosted conversions are priced separately. We give you a firm price before you agree to anything.

Who does the installation?

We handle everything up to the install: checking your eligibility, confirming what the job needs, and giving you the firm quote. Installation itself is done by Ecogenica Pty Ltd, NSW contractor licence 397621C, using its own licensed team rather than a subcontractor, with a 7-year warranty on the tank.

What warranty do I get?

Every tank we install carries a 7-year warranty, on all three models. Because the manufacturer does the installation rather than a subcontractor, warranty questions go to one place instead of being argued between a supplier and an installer.

Am I guaranteed to be eligible?

No. Eligibility is set by the NSW Government and depends on your household income, whether you own the property, and whether your home suits the upgrade. That’s exactly what the eligibility check is for.

Do I have to take the finance?

No. The NSW Home Energy Saver Loan is an option for eligible households, not a condition. Note the minimum loan is $2,000, so a smaller job can fall below the threshold.

Will someone knock on my door?

No. Unsolicited door-knocking to promote these upgrades is banned under the NSW program. You’ll only hear from us because you asked to.
